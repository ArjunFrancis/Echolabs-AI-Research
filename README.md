## 💼 Business Model (2026 Update)

EchoLabs operates a **tri-model business** combining platform, consulting, and audit services:

### **Model 1: Enterprise Platform (SaaS)**
Full-stack LLM/agent evaluation, testing, monitoring, and **UAE AI Act 2026 compliance automation**:
- 🧪 **Evaluation & Testing Engine** - Multi-modal testing (text, vision, audio), reasoning benchmarks, tool-use validation, compliance testing
- 🤖 **Agentic Evaluation** - Multi-agent workflow testing (CrewAI, AutoGen, LangGraph), session-level evaluation, emergent behavior detection
- 📊 **Real-Time Monitoring** - Agent performance tracking, cost/token analytics, error detection, audit trails
- ✅ **UAE AI Act 2026 Compliance Dashboard** 🆕
  - Automated quarterly bias testing with public disclosure generation
  - 72-hour incident reporting with UAE AI Authority integration
  - Annual audit preparation toolkit (evidence packages, model cards)
  - Human-in-the-loop workflow management
  - Risk tier classification (Tier 1-4)
- 📁 **Dataset Management** - Training data versioning, test dataset curation, synthetic data generation
- 🔄 **RLHF Pipeline** - Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback infrastructure

### **Model 2: Consulting-First Services**
Bespoke consulting to bridge knowledge gaps and accelerate compliant AI adoption:
- 📋 **AI Readiness Audits** - Team knowledge assessment, infrastructure evaluation, **UAE AI Act compliance gap analysis** 🆕
- 🎯 **Unbiased LLM/Vendor Selection** - Vendor-neutral evaluation, comparative analysis, **Tier 3-4 compliance fit scoring** 🆕
- 💰 **Pre-Sales ROI Analysis** - ROI calculation, use-case prioritization, risk assessment
- 🎓 **Team Training & Upskilling** - Custom training curriculum, hands-on workshops, progress tracking
- 🎁 **Value-First Lead Generation** - Free AI readiness checklists, LLM selection guides, **UAE AI Act compliance checklists** 🆕

### **Model 3: Accredited Audit Services** 🆕
UAE AI Authority-accredited algorithm audits for Tier 3-4 systems:
- 🔍 **Annual Algorithm Audits** (Tier 3) - Bias/fairness testing, accuracy validation, security assessment
- 🔍 **Quarterly Algorithm Audits** (Tier 4) - Critical-risk systems, continuous monitoring
- 📄 **Compliance Certification** - UAE AI Authority-approved audit reports
- 💡 **Remediation Consulting** - Address audit findings, 90-day compliance roadmap

**Pricing:**
- Tier 3 Annual Audit: **AED 75,000** (30-50% lower than Big 4)
- Tier 4 Quarterly Audit: **AED 150,000-600,000/year**
- Platform Subscription: **AED 25,000-50,000/month**

**Revenue Opportunity:** AED 40M+ from audit services + platform conversions by 2027
